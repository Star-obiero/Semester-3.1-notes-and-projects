n8n: The Open Source Powerhouse for Workflow Automation

In an era where efficiency and integration define business success, automation tools have become indispensable. Among the growing landscape of workflow automation platforms, n8n has emerged as a standout — a flexible, open-source alternative to giants like Zapier, Make (formerly Integromat), and Power Automate.

What Is n8n?

n8n (pronounced “n-eight-n”) is an open-source workflow automation tool that allows users to connect different applications and services to automate repetitive tasks — without needing to write extensive code. Its name stands for “nodemation,” a blend of node and automation.

Unlike many closed, cloud-only automation services, n8n gives users full control: it can be self-hosted, customized, and extended to suit any business or development environment.
